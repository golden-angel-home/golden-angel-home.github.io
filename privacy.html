Privacy policy

Golden Angel is a personal project used by its author's household.

Google account data. It reads and writes one Google Calendar, using the Google Calendar API, to show and manage that household's appointments. Calendar data is kept on the household's own machine. Calendar details may be sent to Google's Gemini service so the software can answer the household's questions. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Retention. Calendar data is held only as long as the household uses the software, and is deleted when access is revoked.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.
